Design Notes

Inspired by royal Rajasthani and Mughal lobby chairs found in five-star Indian heritage hotels. Features include:

  • Scalloped arch back silhouette
  • Jali-inspired lattice side panels
  • Quilted cushion surface with piping
  • Dark carved wood frame with brass/gold trim
  • Decorative stud accents
