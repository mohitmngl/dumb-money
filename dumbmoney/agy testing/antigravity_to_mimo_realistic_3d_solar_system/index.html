About This Demo

This interactive 3D solar system explorer lets you explore the Sun, all eight planets, and detailed moon information for each.

Scale Notice: Distances and sizes are intentionally compressed for educational usability. In reality, the distances between planets are vastly larger relative to their sizes.

Moon Information:

  • Moon counts change as new discoveries are confirmed by astronomers.
  • This demo focuses on major and representative moons for readability.
  • Mercury and Venus have no confirmed natural moons.

Visual Notes:

  • Texture and materials are educational approximations.
  • Saturn's rings are shown with realistic banding patterns.
  • Uranus is depicted with its characteristic axial tilt.

Controls:

  • Left click + drag: Rotate view
  • Scroll wheel: Zoom in/out
  • Right click + drag: Pan
  • Click on body: Select and view information

Built with Three.js • Data for educational purposes
