About Orrery Live

Welcome to Orrery Live, a self-contained interactive web application built to simulate and explore the celestial dynamics of our Solar System.

⚠️ Important Scientific Note on Scale

To make the Solar System viewable and usable on a single screen, both size and distance scales are heavily compressed. In the real universe, if the Earth were the size of a marble, the Sun would be a large exercise ball 100 meters away, and Neptune would be over 3 kilometers away! Orbit rates and celestial body sizes are adjusted relative to each other for visual balance.

Educational Highlights:

  • Sun & 8 Planets: Supports interactive selection and orbit simulation for the star and all major planets.
  • Natural Satellites (Moons): Explore representative moons across the solar system, including Mars' asteroid-like captures, Jupiter's Galilean giants, Saturn's icy spheres, Uranus' Shakespearean satellites, and Neptune's retrograde wonder, Triton.
  • Evolving Astronomy: Moon counts change constantly as astronomers verify new discoveries. This demo highlights the primary and representative moons, pointing out total counts for gas and ice giants.

Navigation & Interaction:

  • Select: Click directly on any celestial body on the screen to view detailed facts.
  • Zoom Focus: Once a planet is selected, click "Zoom & Show Moons" to zoom into its gravitational system and see its moons orbit in detail.
  • Orbit controls: Use your mouse (click and drag) to pan the camera across the Solar System, or use the scroll wheel to zoom in and out.
  • Controls: Adjust orbital speeds, toggle orbits, labels, or twinkling starfields, or pause the system at any time.
