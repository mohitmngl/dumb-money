Solar System Explorer

About This Explorer

Scale: Distances and sizes in this simulation are intentionally compressed for visibility and usability. In reality, the space between planets is vastly larger, and planets are much smaller relative to those distances.

Moons: Moon counts change as discoveries are constantly confirmed. This demo focuses on the major and most representative moons for readability.

Visuals: The textures and materials on celestial bodies are educational approximations generated procedurally in your browser using HTML5 Canvas, requiring no external images.
