Realistic 3D Solar System Explorer

An interactive 3D visualization of our Solar System built with Three.js.

Controls

  • Drag to rotate the camera around the scene
  • Scroll to zoom in and out
  • Right-click drag to pan
  • Click any planet, moon, or the Sun to view detailed information
  • Use the Search bar to quickly find and focus on any celestial body
  • Adjust Speed to control orbital animation rate
  • Toggle Orbits, Labels, and Auto-Rotate
  • Choose different Environment backgrounds

Notes

Planet sizes and orbital distances are not to scale — they are adjusted for visual clarity and educational purposes.

Moon counts represent known confirmed natural satellites. Many outer planets have additional small moons not individually rendered here.

Built with Three.js loaded via CDN.
